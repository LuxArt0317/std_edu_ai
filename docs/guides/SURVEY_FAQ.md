# School FAQ — answers from the consolidated survey

Prepared: **24 November 2026**. Language: English, retaining Chinese menu labels where useful. This file covers all 53 substantive consolidated questions across the 12 survey topics. Empty, joke and off-topic responses are excluded.

## Editorial and source scope

These are composed FAQ answers, not fabricated replies from a signed-in student's records. Public instructions can be read without sign-in; personal amounts, schedules, grades, leave totals and submissions require that person's authorized account. A missing record is not a zero balance, no assignment or a passed requirement.

Student-facing navigation links below point to **existing stu.best route definitions or captured page/asset mappings**. This was a document/source review; live deployment and chatbot testing remain paused. A related page is explicitly called related where it is not the exact supplied source. Source provenance and missing equivalents are recorded at the end.

Public captures used here are dated 3 November 2026. The supplied IPIAC PDF was retrieved and read on 24 November 2026. Historical dates are identified explicitly. **115 academic year means 2026–2027**, not calendar year 2015. Monetary amounts are NT$.

## 1. Tuition, fees and payment

### Q01. How much is tuition, including for IPIAC scholars and international students?

**Answer:** Your program and study level determine the rate. The **115 academic-year undergraduate daytime table** lists tuition and miscellaneous fees of **NT$53,390 per semester** for Information/Design, Child Care and Family Services, and Information Management; the Management/Applied Social Sciences category is **NT$46,433**, subject to the table's department exceptions. International students use the rate for their program. These are tuition-and-miscellaneous totals, not an all-inclusive living-cost bill. [Read the 115 fee table](https://stu.best/school-assets/ae47f1b002b79a4e65d6.pdf) or [browse tuition information](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.acc.stu.edu.tw/%E5%AD%B8%E8%B2%BB%E8%A8%88%E7%AE%97/%E5%AD%B8%E9%9B%9C%E8%B2%BB%E5%B0%88%E5%8D%80/).

For the three IPIAC programs in the supplied 115-entry prospectus—Information Management, Computer and Communication, and Computer Science and Information Engineering—the stated base is **NT$53,390**. It describes a first-semester tuition waiver and later performance-based awards. Those conditions apply to that prospectus, not automatically to every entry year. See Q06–Q08 for the calculation and limitations.

### Q02. What are the payment options and deadlines?

**Answer:** The captured [registration-payment notice](https://stu.best/school-sites/cashier.swd.stu.edu.tw/registration-fee-area/) lists bank-counter payment, Taiwan Pay, ATM bill payment, credit card and convenience-store payment. Convenience stores charge a handling fee. Use the payment details on your own bill; do not send money to an account supplied in chat.

For **115-1 only**, the notice says payment was due **11 September 2026**, with convenience-store payment ending **31 August 2026**. Those dates have passed; they are not the next semester's deadline. Obtain the applicable term's bill before paying. The same notice says credit-card reconciliation takes about three business days and convenience stores about five to six; specified bank/ATM/Taiwan Pay channels reconcile the same day. Receipt availability is described separately, so payment status need not change instantly. For a deadline or reconciliation problem, contact the Cashier, **07-6158000 ext. 2208**.

The exact supplied payment-method page is not in the local catalog. The link above is a related notice with usable instructions; its missing exact equivalent is **[P0 issue #102](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**.

### Q03. Can I see my real-time tuition and fee payment status?

**Answer:** Sign in to [School Affairs](https://stu.best/school-affairs), then check [your notifications](https://stu.best/school-affairs/notifications) and [pending fees](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/39-1). You can ask the assistant to read the fee records linked to your account. Without sign-in, it should explain the process but cannot show a personal balance.

A saved status is only as current as its last update. Compare the term, outstanding amount, payment reference and update time with your receipt; an unposted payment should not be described as unpaid with certainty. Bank settlement may take the time described in Q02. These pages do not by themselves guarantee a live bank connection.

### Q04. What tuition discounts are available?

**Answer:** Check [tuition reduction applications](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/21-3), the [government reduction survey](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/24-1), and [scholarship information](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.oica.stu.edu.tw/%E7%8D%8E%E5%8A%A9%E5%AD%B8%E9%87%91/). Eligibility differs by nationality, study level, entry year and award. The 115-1 payment notice describes a government reduction of up to **NT$17,500 per semester** for specified domestic student categories, with exclusions and restrictions on other government assistance; that is **not a universal international-student discount**. IPIAC support has separate conditions. Confirm the approved reduction on your bill before subtracting it. Student Affairs tuition-reduction enquiries: **07-6158000 ext. 2140**.

## 2. Scholarships

### Q05. Where can I find my scholarship status?

**Answer:** Sign in and open [International Student Scholarship Applications](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/9-1). The menu path is **School Affairs → Academic Affairs (教務系統) → Application/Input Programs (申請輸入程式) → International Students (境外學生) → 境外學生獎助學金申請**. Select the application period and review the saved application or award status. An application awaiting review is not an approved scholarship or a payment. If the period or decision is absent, ask OICA rather than assuming rejection. [OICA contact information](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.oica.stu.edu.tw/%E8%81%AF%E7%B5%A1%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A/).

### Q06. Can you explain my IPIAC scholarship, including different academic years of entry?

**Answer:** I need your **entry academic year, program and scholarship award notice** to identify your rules. The supplied 115-entry prospectus states a first-semester tuition waiver; students living in campus accommodation throughout that semester may receive an **NT$11,000 accommodation subsidy in the following semester**. From semester two, it describes performance-based awards of **NT$20,000, NT$10,000 or NT$3,000**, subject to eligibility.

It does not establish the rules for earlier cohorts. Its appendix also contains mixed 114/115 and 2025 year labels. Older cohort regulations and an exact local copy are urgent gaps in **[issue #102](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**. Use [your saved award](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/9-1) and [OICA](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.oica.stu.edu.tw/%E8%81%AF%E7%B5%A1%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A/) to settle which conditions apply to you; do not transfer the 115 draft to a 112-entry student.

### Q07. How is the tuition deduction for scholars calculated?

**Answer:** Start with the tuition-and-miscellaneous charge, subtract the **approved tuition award**, then add separately billed items and account for payments or credits already posted. For the prospectus's NT$53,390 base, the arithmetic is:

| Approved tuition award | Tuition and miscellaneous amount remaining |
| --- | ---: |
| First-semester full waiver: NT$53,390 | NT$0 |
| NT$20,000 | NT$33,390 |
| NT$10,000 | NT$43,390 |
| NT$3,000 | NT$50,390 |

These calculations exclude accommodation, deposits, insurance and other items; they are **not your final bill**. The NT$11,000 accommodation subsidy described for eligible first-semester residents is paid in semester two and should not automatically be treated as an immediate dorm-fee deduction. Check [your scholarship decision](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/9-1) and [pending fees](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/39-1). The PDF's first-semester subtotal retains the pre-waiver tuition despite listing zero after reduction, so that subtotal is not safe to reuse. **[Correction tracked here](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**.

### Q08. What scholarship programs does the school offer?

**Answer:** Start with the [scholarship directory](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.oica.stu.edu.tw/%E7%8D%8E%E5%8A%A9%E5%AD%B8%E9%87%91/), which separates internal and external opportunities, and [internal international-student scholarships](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.oica.stu.edu.tw/%E6%A0%A1%E5%85%A7%E7%8D%8E%E5%8A%A9%E5%AD%B8%E9%87%91/). The survey's IPIAC prospectus describes the New Southbound IPIAC tuition award, performance tiers and language-test incentives of **NT$2,000 for A2** and **NT$3,000 for B1 or above** under that document's conditions. It does not prove the two language awards can be stacked. Current openings, eligibility, required documents and renewal rules must be read for the individual award. A directory listing does not mean an award has been granted to your account.

### Q09. What are the requirements for an IPIAC scholarship?

**Answer:** In the supplied prospectus, later-semester awards depend on academic performance **and** other requirements. Its tiers are **average at least 85 and top 20% of the class: NT$20,000; average at least 75 and top 50%: NT$10,000; all subjects at least 60: NT$3,000**. These are tier descriptions, not cumulative awards.

The appendix requires campus residence throughout the semester, except approved accommodation arranged through the internship provider/employer; from semester three it requires **TOCFL A2 or higher** and participation in the required internship program. Your cohort's award notice still controls applicability. Submit/check the relevant [application](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/9-1); older-year rules and the exact prospectus link remain **[P0 work](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**.

## 3. Grades, leave and personal academic records

### Q10. How do I check my grades, including Moodle grades?

**Answer:** Sign in and open [School Affairs Grade Inquiry](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/13-1): **Academic Affairs → Query Programs (查詢程式) → Grades (成績) → Grade Inquiry (成績查詢)**. Choose the academic year and semester. For course activities and coursework marks, use [DTM grades](https://stu.best/dtm/grades) and select the course. A DTM activity score and an officially recorded semester grade are different records and may be released at different times. The assistant should say which it read rather than combine them into an invented final grade.

### Q11. Where is the leave (請假) section?

**Answer:** Open the [Student Leave form](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/leave). From the menu: **School Affairs → Student Affairs (學務資訊) → Application/Input Programs → Student Leave (學生請假) → 請假填單**. Choose the new-request option, enter the leave type, dates and class periods, supply any required evidence, and review the request before submitting. You can check it later in [leave history](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/leave/history). “New Fake Orders” in the supplied notes is a machine-translation error, not the name of a separate service. A saved draft must not be described as an approved leave.

### Q12. How many leaves have I already used?

**Answer:** Sign in and open [your leave history](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/leave/history). Specify the semester and whether you mean **requests, approved days or approved class periods**; these totals can differ. The assistant should count the matching saved records and state whether pending, rejected and cancelled requests were excluded. It should not invent a remaining leave allowance when the applicable rule has not been supplied.

### Q13. What is my class schedule?

**Answer:** Open [My Course Schedule](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/11-2) after signing in: **Academic Affairs → Query Programs → Student Course Selection → 我的功課表 → Query**. Select the term and check the course, weekday, period, time, teacher and room shown in your account. For “my next class,” the assistant must compare your selected timetable with the current date and **Taiwan time**, and check any cancellation notice available. A general course catalog does not prove you are enrolled.

### Q14. Can I look up a friend's grades?

**Answer:** Your sign-in lets you see **your own authorized records**, not another student's private grades. Your friend can open [Grade Inquiry](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/13-1) in their own account or choose to share a transcript with you. Knowing their name or student number does not grant access. Staff access must follow the permissions assigned to that staff account.

## 4. Graduation and course planning

### Q15. What are the graduation requirements for my department?

**Answer:** Check [your graduation-credit review](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/15-1) alongside the [curriculum for your entry year](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/12-5). Requirements depend on department, degree and entry year; total credits alone may not cover required subjects, language or other graduation conditions. The assistant should identify satisfied and missing categories from the applicable rules and your records. If the entry-year rules are absent, it should name that gap instead of claiming a standard university-wide threshold. [Missing exact-rule coverage is tracked here](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102).

### Q16. Which classes should I take next semester to meet my credit threshold?

**Answer:** Compare [completed enrollments](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/11-1), [credit review](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/15-1), your [entry-year curriculum](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/12-5) and the [offered-course list](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/12-4). Prioritize missing required courses, prerequisites and remaining credit categories, then check timetable clashes and credit limits. The assistant can propose a course plan from those records, but a proposed subject is not enrolled until the actual registration succeeds. If next semester's offering has not been published, the plan must remain provisional.

### Q17. What are my subjects for a particular year and semester?

**Answer:** Open [Course Selection Records](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/11-1), choose the academic year and semester, and review that term's saved subjects. Use [My Course Schedule](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/11-2) for their times and rooms. Tell the assistant the term you mean, such as **115-1 (2026–2027, semester one)**; it should distinguish completed, currently enrolled and planned subjects, rather than treating every course in DTM or the public catalog as your enrollment.

### Q18. Based on my past grades, can I graduate, and what rank would I get?

**Answer:** Graduation eligibility requires [your grades](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/13-1), [credit review](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/15-1), the correct curriculum and other required clearance conditions. Past marks alone cannot prove graduation eligibility. Class rank additionally needs an authorized official ranking and comparison group; your individual transcript cannot establish the rest of the class's results. The assistant can explain the gaps and any recorded rank, but should not predict a rank as a fact.

### Q19. When is graduation?

**Answer:** The captured **115 academic-year student calendar** lists the graduation ceremony on **12 June 2027**. Ceremony attendance, academic completion and degree issuance are separate events. Check the [student calendar PDF](https://stu.best/school-assets/45b4315881485ac84bff.pdf) for the year and the [graduation-clearance information](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.aca.stu.edu.tw/%E7%95%A2%E6%A5%AD%E9%9B%A2%E6%A0%A1-leaving/) for the relevant graduating semester. The latter capture contains earlier 114-2 processing dates, which should not be reused as the 115-2 clearance deadlines.

### Q20. Can I graduate without attending classes?

**Answer:** You must satisfy the requirements that apply to your program, including earned credits, required activities or placements and any attendance-related course rules. Simply stopping attendance does not complete them. If you already have eligible credits, check [credit review](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/15-1) and the relevant credit-transfer or graduation procedure with your department. If you need to stop studying, ask about formal leave of absence or withdrawal rather than just missing classes; those are different from the [single-class/day leave form](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/leave).

## 5. Moodle / DTM and coursework

### Q21. How do I open or log in to Moodle?

**Answer:** For the **Digital Teaching Materials service described in this survey**, open [DTM](https://stu.best/dtm) or [its sign-in page](https://stu.best/dtm/login) and use Continue with Google. On stu.best, DTM and School Affairs use the same account session on that domain. Choose your course after sign-in. DTM, the separate Digital Academy and TronClass are distinct services; the assistant should ask which one holds your course if it is unclear. Google sign-in identifies your account; it does not grant unrelated students' course records.

### Q22. Do I have pending or missed assignments?

**Answer:** Open [My assignments](https://stu.best/dtm/assignments). The assistant should compare each authorized assignment's deadline, submission status and any extension using Taiwan time, then list **not submitted, overdue, submitted or returned for revision** separately. A submitted activity awaiting grading is not automatically unfinished. If a course or deadline is missing, it should identify the coverage gap rather than say you have nothing due.

### Q23. How do I submit my midterm answers?

**Answer:** In [DTM](https://stu.best/dtm), open the correct course, then the midterm activity. Follow that activity's instructions: a file assignment, quiz and external assessment can require different steps. Check the allowed format and deadline, add your work, complete the submission/confirmation step if shown, and verify the saved status or receipt. An upload preview or draft is not final submission. The assistant needs the exact activity to give a precise link, and should not claim it submitted an exam without a successful supported action and your required confirmation.

### Q24. Is there class today, and when is the midterm?

**Answer:** Check [your timetable](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/11-2), the course's [DTM announcements and activities](https://stu.best/dtm), and the [academic calendar](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.aca.stu.edu.tw/%E8%A1%8C%E4%BA%8B%E6%9B%86%EF%BD%9Ccalendar/). The 115-1 general midterm period was **2–8 November 2026**; the 115-2 calendar lists **19–25 April 2027**. A course's exact exam time can differ within the published arrangements, so use the teacher's activity notice for the exact session. A timetable entry alone does not rule out a cancellation.

### Q25. Can you recommend books or videos to supplement my learning?

**Answer:** Tell me the subject and topic, or point me to the [DTM course](https://stu.best/dtm). I can start with its reading list, syllabus and teacher-provided materials, then explain which resources match what you are studying. Any extra recommendation should be identified as a suggestion, with its actual title/source and appropriate level; it should not be invented or described as required by your teacher without evidence.

In the captured 115 calendar, **Library Week runs 30 November–6 December 2026**, and the **graduation ceremony is 12 June 2027**. Those are examples from the published academic calendar, not a complete live list of all campus events. Check each event notice for registration, place and changes before attending.

The assistant should list only requirements established by those rules, show the recorded completion and identify missing evidence. It should not assume every club event is compulsory or treat a missing attendance entry as a proven absence.

The [115 student PDF](https://stu.best/school-assets/45b4315881485ac84bff.pdf) is the 2026–2027 version. Personal assignment due dates still come from the activity in DTM, not just the university calendar.

## 7. Dormitory

### Q30. What are the dorm fees, and how do international students apply?

**Answer:** The captured **115 fee table** lists these per-semester accommodation rates, excluding winter/summer breaks:

| Residence / room | Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| First dorm, six-person non-ensuite | NT$10,000 |
| First dorm, three- or four-person ensuite | NT$13,000 |
| Second dorm | NT$10,000 |
| Third dorm | NT$11,000 |
| Wenhui Hall | NT$16,000 |

It lists an **NT$3,500 deposit per academic year**, with air-conditioning and network charges governed separately. [Read the fee table](https://stu.best/school-assets/ae47f1b002b79a4e65d6.pdf). The supplied IPIAC prospectus's separate cost estimate mentions NT$11,000 six-person / NT$16,000 four-person accommodation and a first-check-in deposit, so it should not be treated as a universal dorm tariff. Confirm your assigned residence and program arrangement.

Apply through [Student Accommodation](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/26-1), then check [bed allocation](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/34-1). International/IPIAC students should coordinate any program-arranged accommodation with OICA. Housing enquiries: **07-6158000 ext. 2136**. A saved application is not a confirmed room allocation.

### Q31. How and when can I move out of the dorm?

**Answer:** Arrange checkout with the residence office before leaving: confirm the applicable deadline, room/key inspection, outstanding charges and deposit process. The captured 115 student calendar says term-end closure is **11 January 2027 before 10:00**, and **28 June 2027 before 10:00** for semester two. These are term-end dates, not permission for early termination. IPIAC residents should check scholarship accommodation conditions before moving off campus. Exact early move-out forms and refund rules still need a confirmed local instruction page in **[P0 issue #102](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**. Start with [your accommodation record](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/34-1) and residence support, ext. **2136**.

### Q32. How do I report a broken light and schedule a repair?

**Answer:** Open [Online Repair](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/45-1), identify the dorm/building, room and faulty light, describe when the issue occurs, and add any requested evidence or contact availability. Keep the saved request/reference and check its status. A reported fault does not mean a technician's visit is booked; use the recorded appointment or residence-office confirmation for that. If there is sparking, exposed wiring or another immediate hazard, avoid the fixture and notify the residence staff promptly rather than waiting for a routine online request.

### Q33. How do I get dorm Wi-Fi on my laptop?

**Answer:** The [wireless guide](https://stu.best/school-sites/www.ecc.stu.edu.tw/wireless/) says dorm wireless service requires registration through the dorm network system. After that, use the [device-specific setup guide](https://stu.best/school-sites/help.ecc.stu.edu.tw/wireless-setting/): choose computer, Windows/macOS and dorm. It recommends the 802.1X networks **STU-Dorm-Mobile** or **STU-Dorm-Mobile-5G**, depending on the device. Do not invent a shared campus-wide password. The [dorm network guide](https://stu.best/school-sites/freshman.stu.edu.tw/2020/05/13/%E5%AE%BF%E8%88%8D%E7%B6%B2%E8%B7%AF/) also explains that wired service uses the IP assigned to your bed; do not use someone else's IP.

The exact dorm-registration page has no local catalog equivalent yet: **[P0 issue #102](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**. The local instruction pages above are not themselves network registration. For connection problems, the wireless page lists **07-6158000 ext. 2420**, **service@stu.edu.tw**, or [online repair](https://stu.best/school-affairs/services/45-1).

### Q39. How do I connect to school Wi-Fi?

**Answer:** Open the [Wi-Fi setup guide](https://stu.best/school-sites/help.ecc.stu.edu.tw/wireless-setting/) and choose your device, operating system and campus location. ### Important source conflicts and incomplete answers

- **IPIAC year and subtotal conflicts:** Physical PDF p. 29 mixes 114/115 and “2025” labels. Its semester-one subtotal NT$78,248 equals base tuition + dorm + other fees before the displayed waiver; the stated waived balance cannot be derived from that subtotal. Later subtotals likewise precede performance-based deductions. The arithmetic in Q07 uses the explicit tuition/award values instead.
- **Insurance and dorm estimates differ:** The IPIAC cost estimate lists student insurance of NT$878, while the 115 Accounting fee table lists NT$846. Dorm estimates and deposit timing differ too. Do not present the draft's “other fees” column as the final current bill; obtain the student's term bill and cohort rules.
- **Dates are scoped:** Payment and transcript reservations above are historical. Calendar dates come from the captured 115 student edition approved 15 April 2026; later announcements may revise them.
- **Unverified exact procedures:** Lost property, student-card replacement fee/process, current admissions windows, current internal staff training registration, dorm early move-out, bank/form integrations and student messaging lack complete exact-source evidence. The FAQ states the limitation and an actionable contact rather than fabricating a result. These are consolidated under **[P0 #102](https://github.com/LuxArt0317/std_edu_ai/issues/102)**.
- **Account questions:** Q03, Q05, Q12–Q13, Q15–Q18, Q22, Q24 and Q27 need account data plus applicable rules. This static FAQ intentionally contains no invented personal result. Its answers are reusable guidance until a verified signed-in lookup supplies the values.
