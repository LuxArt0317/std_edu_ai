第壹章、緒論/ Chapter 1: Introduction

第一節、研究背景與動機/ Section 1: Research Background and Motivation

        人工智慧（Artificial Intelligence, AI）已成為現代社會最具影響力的科技之一，徹底改變了人們取得、處理及管理資訊的方式。在教育領域中，人工智慧技術近年來已逐漸整合至各種數位服務，以提升使用者獲取資訊的便利性。目前，大多數教育機構的數位基礎建設仍以傳統靜態網站架構為主，使用者必須透過多層式選單逐步搜尋所需資訊，因此往往需要花費大量時間瀏覽不同網頁及資料。透過人工智慧輔助平台，學生將能更快速且有效率地取得所需資訊，減少在不同網頁及文件間搜尋資訊所耗費的時間與精力。
    Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become one of the most powerful technologies developed in modern society which transformed the way people access, process, and manage information. In educational sectors, these technologies are recently being heavily integrated into digital services to improve the information accessibility of users. The current digital infrastructure of educational institutions heavily relies on static website architectures which makes these traditional websites depend on multi-layered menus that require users to manually search through overwhelming directories. Through AI-assisted platforms, students will be able to obtain information quickly and efficiently, reducing the wasted time and effort required to search through multiple webpages and documents.
在教育數位化發展中，導入人工智慧資訊助理的重要性在於能有效協助學生、教師及校外訪客解決使用大學網站時所面臨的各種困難。例如招生資訊、畢業資格、行政單位位置、學術規定等重要資訊，通常分散於不同的網頁中，使資訊查找過程變得耗時且容易造成使用者困擾。因此，學生往往需要向行政人員反覆詢問相同問題，不僅增加行政負擔，也降低服務效率。若能透過具有自然對話功能的 AI 聊天機器人直接提供答案，將有助於改善使用者體驗、提升資訊可及性，並增進學生與學校之間的溝通效率。
  The relevance of using AI-powered information assistants in the digital education sector lies behind the benefits it contributes to the students, faculty members, and visitors encountering difficulties on university websites. Important details including and not limited to enrollment procedures, graduation requirements, office locations, and academic regulations are commonly distributed across multiple webpages. This type of digital architecture makes information retrieval time-consuming and frustrating. As a result, students may increasingly grow confused and dependent on administrative staff for routine inquiries. By providing a conversational interface that delivers direct responses, AI chatbots will be able to have a positive impact on user experience, accessibility and communication between students and educational institutions.
許多研究指出，在大學網站導入 AI 資訊檢索系統能有效提升使用者體驗。世界各地的大學正積極推動「智慧校園（Smart Campus）」建設，透過新興科技改善學習環境與行政服務品質。Leem 與 Kim（2021）指出，數位科技、資料分析及智慧系統是智慧校園發展的三大核心支柱，可有效提升學校整體營運效率。此外，許多高等教育機構已開始導入各種 AI 技術，以協助學生處理學術及行政相關問題。Polin 等人（2023）亦指出，高等教育機構逐漸認知到智慧系統能提供更快速、更個人化的資訊服務。
Several studies have shown that AI-assisted information retrieval systems on university websites can improve user experience. Universities around the world are starting a major digital information effort often called “Smart Campus” which uses new technologies to improve learning environments and administrative services. According to a study by Leem and Kim (2021), digital technologies, data analytics, and intelligent systems are the three main pillars of integrating a smart campus that enhances institutional efficiency. Universities across different countries have also begun implementing various AI-driven services to assist users with both academic and administrative concerns. Polin et al. (2023) noted that these developments show that higher education institutions are increasingly recognizing the value of intelligent systems in providing faster and more personalized access to information. 
近年來，臺灣教育部積極推動高等教育數位轉型，尤其透過「高等教育深耕計畫（Higher Education Sprout Project）」鼓勵大專校院導入人工智慧、雲端運算及大數據分析等新興技術，以提升教育品質及行政效率。此外，隨著《人工智慧基本法》於 2026 年正式推動，政府機關及學術機構在系統設計上更需重視使用者自主權、資訊可近性及友善介面。然而，目前仍有許多大學網站採用傳統資訊架構，資訊主要依照行政單位分類，而非依據學生需求設計。對於不熟悉校內組織架構的新生或訪客而言，往往需要花費大量時間才能找到所需資訊。因此，高等教育機構更需要建立以使用者為中心的資訊服務模式，以提升資訊管理效率與使用者滿意度。
The importance of digital transformation in higher education has been seen to be slowly reinforced in Taiwan through initiatives promoted by the Ministry of Education particularly via the Higher Education Sprout Project (2023), which ties integration of emerging technologies to university fundings. This specific initiative encourages the adoption of emerging technologies including artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and big data analysis to improve the overall quality of education and efficiency in an administration. Furthermore, with the passage of the Artificial Intelligence Basic Act (2026), public sectors and academic institutions in Taiwan are legally obliged to integrate user autonomy and accessibility directly into system designs. Despite these statutory efforts, a number of universities are still relying on structures with traditional navigation systems that organize information according to departments rather than the needs of the students. Consequently, when students are especially unfamiliar with the organizational structure of a university they will often have a much higher chance of struggling to locate important information efficiently. This situation stresses the importance of having a more user-centered approach to information management in higher education.
在樹德科技大學，學生經常透過學校官方網站查詢學術規定、選課資訊、招生流程、畢業資格及各項行政服務。然而，由於網站內容龐大且資訊分散於不同頁面，使用者仍可能面臨資訊查找困難。Davis 與 Smith（2025）指出，教育行政單位在開學及選課等尖峰期間，經常收到大量重複性的學生詢問，例如註冊程序、畢業規定及各項行政流程，導致行政人員工作負擔增加，同時降低整體行政效率。
At Shu-Te University, students are regularly relying on the university website when accessing information regarding academic policies, procedures for enrollment, requirements for graduation, and even administrative services. However, users may encounter a few difficulties due to the large number of information that is being distributed across many webpages. Davis and Smith (2025) states that educational administrative offices often receive repetitive inquiries from students during any peak academic periods, which in most cases, are usually only regarding common procedures and requirements. In turn, the staff workload significantly increases while also reducing operational efficiency.
因此，本研究旨在建置一套AI強化之樹德科技大學智慧校園與網站系統（AI-Enhanced Shu-Te University Campus & Website），透過智慧資訊檢索技術與人工智慧輔助服務，提供學生及其他使用者更快速、更準確且更容易取得的校園資訊。研究團隊相信，此系統能有效改善網站使用體驗、降低資訊搜尋困難，並進一步促進高等教育機構的數位轉型發展。
Overall, this study aims to develop an AI-enhanced campus and website system for Shu-Te University by integrating intelligent information retrieval and services with AI-assistance. This proposed system seeks to provide accurate, reliable, and easily accessible information to students and other stakeholders. The researchers believe that implementing an AI-assisted information retrieval system can improve user experience, reduce information-searching difficulties, and contribute to the ongoing digital transformation of higher education institutions.











第二節、研究目的 / Section 2: Research Objectives
              本研究旨在探討如何將人工智慧（Artificial Intelligence, AI）應用於樹德科技大學官方網站，以提升智慧校園整體的使用體驗與服務品質。
This study aims to examine how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be applied to enhance the official website of Shu-Te University and improve the overall smart campus user experience.
研究總目標
   設計、開發並實作一套 AI 強化之樹德科技大學智慧校園與網站系統（AI-Enhanced Shu-Te University Campus & Website），提升學校數位服務與校園相關資訊的可用性（Usability）、使用效率（Efficiency）及整體使用者體驗（User Experience）。
To design, develop, and implement an AI-enhanced campus and website system that improves the  usability, efficiency and overall user experience of the digital and campus-related services at Shu-Te University.
具體研究目標
分析樹德科技大學官方網站目前在資訊可及性（Accessibility）、網站可用性（Usability）以及整體使用者體驗等方面的現況。
探討使用者於瀏覽樹德科技大學官方網站時所遭遇的常見問題，包括資訊搜尋效率不足、資訊不易查找，以及互動功能有限等情形。
評估可應用於大學網站的人工智慧技術，例如 AI 聊天機器人（AI Chatbot）、自動化支援服務，以及智慧資訊檢索系統，以改善網站服務品質。
設計並開發一套 AI 強化之樹德科技大學智慧校園與網站系統，整合智慧資訊檢索、AI 聊天機器人、智慧導覽功能及學生支援服務，提供更完善的數位校園體驗。
透過使用者問卷調查等評估方法，分析所開發系統在使用者滿意度、資訊取得速度及降低行政工作負擔等方面的預期效益。

To analyze the current status of Shu-Te University school website in terms of accessibility, usability, and overall user experience.
To identify the common problems encountered by users when accessing the Shu-Te University official website particularly in search efficiency, difficulty in finding information, and limited user interaction features.
To determine possible artificial intelligence applications that can be used to improve the university website, such as AI chatbot for student inquiries, automated support services, and intelligent information retrieval system.
To design and develop an AI-enhanced Shu-Te University campus and website system through implementing an intelligent information retrieval, AI chatbot assistance, smart navigation features, and student support services.
To evaluate the expected benefits of the developed AI-enhanced system in terms of user experience satisfaction, faster information access, and reduced administrative workload using appropriate user surveys as evaluation methods.















第三節、研究範圍與架構 / Section 3: Research Scope and Framework
研究範圍與限制 
	 本研究旨在設計與開發一套 AI 強化秀德科技大學（Shu-Te University）校園與網站系統，著重於提升與學校相關數位服務的整體可及性（Accessibility）、效率（Efficiency）以及使用者體驗（User Experience）。透過人工智慧技術，該系統將協助使用者更有效率地查詢學術與行政相關資訊，並整合自然語言處理（Natural Language Processing, NLP）、檢索增強生成（Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RAG）及聊天機器人（Chatbot）等技術，提供智慧化資訊服務。 
This research is aimed at designing and developing an AI-enhanced Shu-Te University campus and website system that focuses on improving the overall accessibility, efficiency, and user experience of digital services related to the university. 	Through artificial intelligence technologies, the system is intended to help users with efficient academic and administrative information retrieval, including Natural Language Processing, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and chatbot-based assistance.
	雖然大學官方網站通常提供大量資訊，但 Modern Campus 與 Levitz（2023）的研究指出，大學生在使用大學網站時最常遇到的問題包括「資訊難以查找（hard to find information）」及「網站難以瀏覽（hard to navigate）」。這顯示使用者在面對結構複雜的大學網站時，往往難以快速找到所需資訊，尤其是在資訊分散於不同系所、行政單位及多個網頁的情況下，更容易降低資訊取得的效率。
Although university websites normally provide a wide range of information, a study by Modern Campus and Levitz (2023) highlights that the top frustrations among college students are “hard to find information” and “hard to navigate”, emphasizing the difficulty in using complex university website structures, locating specific information, and efficiently accessing information from administrative services. These challenges are an especially important matter in large institutional websites where information is being widely distributed across departments and multiple pages. 
	因此，本研究擬開發一套 AI 輔助平台，透過建立集中式智慧查詢介面，簡化學生與秀德科技大學官方網站之間的互動，使使用者能更快速且方便地取得與學校相關的資訊。 
	Therefore, the researchers aim to develop an AI-assisted platform that will simplify the interaction between students and the Shu-Te University website through developing a centralized intelligence interface for querying information related to the university.
	本研究的範圍包括開發與建置一套 AI 強化資訊系統，能夠處理使用者的提問，並針對學術流程、入學申請、畢業相關資訊、校園服務以及行政單位資訊等提供相關回覆。系統將整合 AI 聊天機器人、智慧資訊檢索功能，以及基本校園導航支援，以協助使用者更容易存取校園資源與官方資訊。 
	The scope of this study will include the development and implementation of an AI-enhanced system that can process user inquiries and provide responses that are relevant to academic procedures, enrollment requirements, graduation information, campus services, and administrative office details. The system will integrate an AI chatbot, functions with intelligent information retrieval, and basic support for campus navigation that will assist users in accessing university resources easily.
	研究團隊將此系統定位為資訊查詢與支援工具，而非決策或行政作業處理系統。因此，本系統不會修改任何官方校務資料，也不會取代行政人員的重要職責，而是作為一個智慧化介面層（Intelligent Interface Layer），提升現有校園資訊的可及性與使用便利性。 
The researchers intend to design the system as an informational and support tool rather than a decision-making or administrative processing system. It will not modify official university records or replace important administrative staff responsibilities.Instead, it will serve as an intelligent interface layer that improves both accessibility and usability of existing university information.
       此外，本系統將建置一個資訊資料庫，內容涵蓋學術政策、系所資訊、校園設施及常見問題（Frequently Asked Questions, FAQs）。為了將使用者需求與資料庫內容進行有效配對，AI 模組將利用自然語言理解（Natural Language Understanding, NLU）技術分析使用者輸入，以提供更準確且符合語境（Context-aware）的回應。 
Furthermore, the system will be structured as an information database that includes academic policies, departmental information, campus facilities, and frequently asked questions. To match the user intent with relevant database content, the AI module will process user queries using natural language understanding to ensure accurate and context-aware responses.
	本研究的目標使用者包括但不限於秀德科技大學的學生，特別是經常需要透過學校官方網站查詢學術及行政資訊的使用者。本系統將提供友善且直覺的互動方式，協助使用者減少搜尋資訊所需的時間與精力，提升整體使用體驗。 
The target users of this research will include but are not limited to students of Shu-Te University, particularly who have to frequently access both academic and administrative information through the official website. The system will support user-friendly interaction and is intended to reduce the time and effort required to locate important information.
然而，本研究不包含進階預測分析（Predictive Analytics）、課程推薦系統（Course Recommendation System）、即時行政業務處理（Real-time Administrative Processing），以及整體校務系統的全面自動化。此外，本系統亦不會取代學校正式的行政決策流程或任何行政權限。研究所提出的系統將僅限於資訊檢索、使用者協助及校園導航支援，藉此改善並提升秀德科技大學官方網站的數位使用體驗。 
However, this research does not include advanced predictive analytics, course recommendation systems, real-time administrative processing, or full automation of the overall institutional system. It also does not replace official university decision-making processes or any form of administrative authority. The proposed system will be entirely limited to information retrieval, user assistance, and campus navigation support to support the digital experience of the university website.
 研究架構 
本研究採用輸入－處理－輸出（Input–Process–Output, IPO）架構，以說明所提出之 AI 強化樹德科技大學校園與網站系統的架構與運作。 
This research adopts the Input–Process–Output (IPO) framework to illustrate the structure and operation of the proposed AI-enhanced Shu-te University campus and website system.

These data sources can be obtained from the official website of Shu-Te University and any associated institutional information systems to ensure accuracy and relevance.
處理 
      處理階段包含一系列由人工智慧驅動的模組：
The processing stage involves a series of AI-powered modules:
自然語言處理（NLP）模組

第四節、研究流程 / Section 4: Research Process

本節提供樹德科技大學 AI 強化校園與網站系統的詳細流程，採用反覆式系統設計、實作與評估作為系統化開發方法的基礎。此流程不僅著重於網站優化，更聚焦於建構一個智慧型資訊支援平台，以更有效地協助使用者查找學術、行政及校園相關資訊。 
This section provides the detailed process for the AI-enhanced Shu-Te University Campus & Website which uses iterative system design, implementation, and evaluation as a basis for a systematic development approach. Rather than focusing only on the enhancement of the website, the process concentrates on constructing an intelligent information support platform that is able to assist in locating academic, administrative, and campus-related information more efficiently.
            第一階段著重於問題識別與系統需求分析。在此階段，研究人員透過檢視現有樹德科技大學網站，找出其在可用性、導覽困難及資訊可及性方面的問題。同時，也納入潛在使用者（如學生、教職員及一般大眾）的回饋，以確定系統需求與使用者期望。經常被查詢的資源，如入學申請流程、畢業要求、系所資訊、辦公室位置及校園服務等，被確認為主要知識領域。
Phase one centered on problem identification and analysis of the system requirements. During this stage, the researchers identified usability issues, navigation difficulties, and limitations in information accessibility by examining the current Shu-Te University website. Feedback from possible users like students, faculty members and even the general public is also considered to determine the required system and user expectations. Resources that are frequently accessed such as enrollment procedures, graduation requirements, departmental information, office locations, and campus services were identified as primary knowledge domains.

	在確定資訊領域後，第二階段進行相關文獻回顧，並進一步建立知識庫。研究團隊參考人工智慧、自然語言處理、檢索增強生成（RAG）及智慧校園系統等重要研究，以建立穩固的理論基礎。同時，從樹德科技大學官方網站取得相關資訊，進行整理與結構化處理，並整合為集中式資料庫。在此過程中，也檢視並移除重複內容與過時資料，以確保系統資料的完整性與正確性。

	After identifying the information domains, the second phase involved reviewing related literature and then proceeding to the knowledge base construction. Important studies on Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and smart campus systems were also reviewed to establish a strong theoretical foundation. Information from the official Shu-Te University website was obtained, organized, and transformed into a structured repository and was consolidated into a centralized database. Any redundant content and outdated records were reviewed to maintain data integrity throughout the system.

	在完成分析後，流程轉向官方 AI 強化平台的開發。令 Q_User 表示使用者提交的自然語言查詢，系統使用自然語言處理技術來處理 Q_User，以識別使用者意圖並擷取相關的語境資訊。處理後的查詢再與機構資料庫 D_STU 進行比較，以擷取最重要的資訊 R_Context。此平台層作為檢索增強生成（RAG）框架的基礎，確保所生成的回應來自經過驗證的大學資訊，而非僅依賴語言模型的預測。 

	Once the analysis has been done, the process shifted to the development of the official AI-enhanced platform. Let Q_User represent a natural language query submitted by a user, The system processes the Q_User using techniques from Natural Language Processing to identify user intent and extract the relevant contextual information. The processed query is then compared to the institutional repository D_STU to retrieve the most important information R_Context. This layer of the platform serves as the foundation of the Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework which ensures that the generated responses are verified university information rather than being solely based on language model predictions.

            在檢索開發完成後，研究人員整合 AI 聊天機器人模組以提供互動式協助。該聊天機器人將所擷取的語境資訊 R_context 與使用者查詢 Q_user 結合，以生成連貫且具情境感知的回應。此方法使使用者能透過對話式語言與大學資訊互動，而非手動瀏覽多個網頁與選單結構。該聊天機器人設計用於支援有關學術流程、行政服務、入學要求、畢業規定及校園設施的查詢。 

Following retrieval development, the researchers integrated an AI chatbot module to provide interactive assistance. The chatbot combines the retrieved contextual information R_context with the user query Q_user to generate coherent and context-aware responses. This approach allows users to interact with university information through conversational language instead of manually navigating multiple webpages and menu structures. The chatbot was designed to support inquiries regarding academic procedures, administrative services, enrollment requirements, graduation policies, and campus facilities. 

	第四階段涉及系統開發與實作。研究人員整合 AI 聊天機器人模組以提供互動式協助。該聊天機器人透過結合擷取的語境資訊 R_Context 與使用者查詢 Q_User，生成連貫且具情境感知的回應。使用對話式語言取代手動瀏覽多個網頁與選單結構的方法，將使使用者能以可能更高的效率與大學資訊互動。 

The fourth phase involved system development and implementation. The researchers integrated an AI chatbot module to provide assistance that is interactive. The chatbot generates coherent and context-aware responses by combining the retrieved contextual information R_Context with the user query Q_User. The approach of using conversational language instead of manually navigating multiple webpages and menu structures will allow users to interact with university information with a possible improved efficiency. 

	第五階段涉及校園導航支援功能的實作。系統資料庫中納入了與位置相關的資訊，例如行政辦公室、服務中心及校園設施。當使用者提交與導航相關的請求時，系統會立即辨識其目標地點，並擷取相應的位置資訊，以協助使用者更有效率地找到校園設施。 

	The fifth phase involved the implementation of support features for campus navigation. Location-related information such as administrative offices, service centers, and campus facilities was incorporated into the system database. When navigation-related requests are submitted by a user, the system immediately identifies the intended destination and retrieves any corresponding location information that will enable users to locate university facilities efficiently.

接著，流程進入系統整合與實作階段。聊天機器人模組、檢索引擎、校園資訊資料庫以及導航組件的整合被轉化為一個統一平台。模組之間的資料傳輸透過一系列測試查詢進行驗證，這些查詢代表常見的學生請求，以確認使用者輸入能被正確處理，且相關資訊能從資料庫中成功擷取。此階段重點在於驗證大學資料庫之間的互動與資料流，而非單獨測試各個模組。測試輸入會通過整個系統流程，以確保資料庫能成功傳輸，並正確將語境資料傳遞至聊天機器人，同時確保聊天機器人能在不遺失或扭曲資訊的情況下輸出回應。此方法確保所有模組皆被正確連接，並以系統整體方式協同運作。 

Moving on, the process shifted to system integration and implementation. The combination of the chatbot module, retrieval engine, database of campus information, and navigation components were transformed into a unified platform. Data communication between modules was tested through a series of test queries that represented common student requests to confirm that user inputs were processed correctly and that relevant information was retrieved from the database. The focus was placed on verifying the interaction and data flow between the University Database instead of testing each module separately. Test inputs were passed through the entire system pipeline to ensure that the database could successfully transmit, correctly pass contextual data to the chatbot, and that the chatbot could deliver responses without loss or distortion of information. This approach ensured every module was connected properly and functioning cohesively as a system.

           最後階段透過使用者問卷調查對所開發的系統進行評估，以衡量其知覺有用性、易用性、資訊可及性及整體滿意度。所獲得之回饋經分析後，用以識別可能的改進方向，並作為系統最終定稿與研究結果文件化之依據。
The final phase involved the evaluation of the developed system through user surveys that assess perceived usefulness, ease of use, information accessibility, and overall satisfaction. Feedback obtained was analyzed to identify possible improvements before the finalized development of the system and documentation of the research findings.

第五節、預期效益 / Section 5: Expected Benefits
   	本研究所開發之 AI 強化樹德科技大學智慧校園與網站系統（AI-Enhanced Shu-Te University Campus & Website），預期將提升學生、教師、行政人員及校外訪客使用學校官方網站的整體體驗，提供更快速、更便利且更有效率的資訊服務。
The AI-Enhanced Shu-Te University Campus & Website system, developed by this research, is anticipated to elevate the overall experience for students, faculty, administrative staff, and external visitors utilizing the university's official website. It will furnish quicker, more convenient, and more efficient information services.
對學生/For Students： 
	本系統協助學生更容易在大學官方網站上查找資訊。透過改善網站導覽功能及使用者友善的介面，學生能夠快速取得公告、課程時間表及課程資訊等學術與行政相關資訊。此系統可減少學生在搜尋資訊時所需花費的時間與精力。 
This system will facilitate students in more easily locating information on the university's official website. Through improved website navigation and a user-friendly interface, students will be able to promptly access academic and administrative information such as announcements, course timetables, and curriculum details. This system will reduce the time and effort students will need to expend when searching for information.
對行政人員/For Administrative Staff： 
	本系統可減少有關招生、註冊及校務程序等重複性詢問的數量。透過即時回覆常見問題，本系統可協助行政人員節省時間，並提升其日常工作效率。 
This system will diminish the volume of repetitive inquiries pertaining to admissions, registration, and university administrative procedures. By providing real-time responses to frequently asked questions, this system will assist administrative staff in conserving time and enhancing the efficiency of their daily tasks.
對教師/For faculty： 
	教師可減少處理學生例行性問題的次數，尤其是與課程時間表及課程要求相關的問題。這使教師能夠將更多時間專注於教學及學術工作。 
Faculty members will experience a reduction in the number of routine student queries they need to address, particularly those related to course timetables and course requirements. This will enable faculty to allocate more time to teaching and academic pursuits.
對樹德科技大學/For Shu-Te University： 
	本校將受益於經改善的官方網站，使網站具有更完善的組織性、更高的可及性及更佳的回應能力。這將提升學生、教職員及訪客所使用之整體數位服務品質。 
The university will benefit from an enhanced official website, which will be characterized by superior organization, increased accessibility, and improved responsiveness. This will elevate the overall quality of digital services utilized by students, faculty, staff, and visitors.
對未來研究者/For Future Researchers： 
	本研究可作為未來與人工智慧、網站系統及使用者體驗設計相關研究之參考。本研究亦提供一套基本架構，可作為發展類似系統之依據。 
This research will serve as a valuable reference for future studies related to artificial intelligence, website systems, and user experience design. Furthermore, this study will provide a foundational framework that can be utilized as a basis for developing similar systems.
