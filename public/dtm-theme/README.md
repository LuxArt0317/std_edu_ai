# DTM theme assets

- `page.svg` and `url.svg` are the activity icons from [Moodle 3.11](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/tree/MOODLE_311_STABLE/mod). Their vector paths match the university's assets.
- `user-small.png` and `user-large.png` are the default [Aardvark avatars](https://github.com/superawesomeme/moodle-theme_aardvark/tree/master/pix_core/u). [GPL license](Moodle-COPYING.txt).
- `open-sans-latin.woff2` is the Latin subset of the Open Sans regular face used by the university theme. [Open Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/opensans), [OFL license](Open-Sans-OFL.txt).
