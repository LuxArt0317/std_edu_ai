【活動公告｜Event Announcement】 統一企業就業面試暨說明會 Uni-President Employment Information & Interview Session

統一企業就業面試暨說明會 Uni-President Employment Information & Interview Session

想了解企業工作機會、職涯發展及實際工作內容嗎？
想直接與企業主管交流，探索適合自己的工作機會嗎？

Are you interested in learning more about job opportunities, career development, and working at Uni-President?
Would you like to meet company managers in person and explore potential job opportunities?

國際生留臺就業輔導基地邀請 統一企業 到校辦理就業說明及交流活動，並邀請直營店主管到場，與有意願的同學進行現場互動及面談，提供同學與企業直接交流及初步媒合的機會。

The International Student Employment Guidance Base is pleased to invite Uni-President Enterprises Corporation to campus for an employment information and networking session. Store managers from Uni-President's directly operated stores will also be present to interact with students and conduct on-site interviews, providing an opportunity to learn about job opportunities and connect directly with the company.

? 活動時間｜Date & Time

115年10月15日（四）12:00–13:00
October 15, 2026 (Thursday), 12:00–13:00

? 活動地點｜Venue

管理大樓 T0304教室
T0304 Classroom, Management Building

? 活動內容｜What to Expect
  • 企業工作機會及職涯說明
    Introduction to job opportunities and career development at Uni-President
  • 直營店主管現場交流
    Networking and interaction with store managers
  • 學生與企業主管現場面談
    On-site interviews with company managers
  • 職涯探索與工作媒合
    Career exploration and potential job matching
? 簡易履歷表｜Simple Resume

有意願參與企業媒合及面談的同學，請事先填寫附件中的簡易履歷表，並於活動當日攜帶至現場，提供企業主管參考，以利進行交流及媒合。

Students who are interested in job matching and on-site interviews are encouraged to complete the attached Simple Resume Form in advance and bring it with you to the event. The resume will be provided to company representatives for reference during the networking and interview session.

? 歡迎對統一企業工作機會、門市工作、職涯發展或留臺就業有興趣的同學踴躍參加！

? International students who are interested in job opportunities, store positions, career development, or working in Taiwan are welcome to join us!

⭐ 把握機會，直接與企業面對面！ ⭐ Meet the company in person and take the first step toward your career!
