College of Applied Social Sciences

To guide students in practice and application to promote students' understanding and related knowledge towards familial and social needs, and further to improve life quality. The college also aims to develop students' awareness and tolerance of diverse cultures, to offer students opportunities to participate in social activities, to focus on whole-person development, to integrate theory and practice, to build up students' self-confidence and self-esteem, and to link theoretical and practical learning to eliminate the gap between academics and application, as well as building up the knowledge base of human and social science, to deepen students' knowledge in foreign languages, family and communities, and sexuality, and to strengthen their abilities in socio-economic application and service. The college is also in pursuit of conducting research and developing knowledge across different fields and countries to reach the outside world for global interchanges.

College of Liberal Education

Liberal education at STU is based upon "holistic education" ideals with courses designed to cultivate students in accordance with the school motto: academics, intellect, happiness, and hope. Students are expected to develop fundamental knowledge and skills in humanity and science, cross-disciplinary reasoning, creative thinking and problem solving through a variety of courses, forums, and activities. Students hence will become well rounded citizens of new ages equipped with both knowledge and practice.​
