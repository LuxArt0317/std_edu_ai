▌註冊須知 Registration Information

內容涵蓋註冊繳費、選課、學雜費減免、就學貸款、弱勢助學、車輛通行證、交通車、住宿，以及兵役相關事項（含緩徵與儘後召集）。請同學依個人需求詳加閱讀，並務必於規定期限內完成各項手續與繳費。

The information includes tuition and fee payment, course selection, tuition and fee reduction programs, student loans, financial aid, vehicle permits, shuttle services, dormitory arrangements, and military service matters, including deferment and call-up adjustments. Students should review the information thoroughly and complete all required procedures and payments within the designated deadlines.
