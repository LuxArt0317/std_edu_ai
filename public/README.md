# Public assets

Vite copies this directory into the site root. `favicon.png` is the 150 × 150 university crest advertised as the favicon on the official `www.stu.edu.tw` homepage; it is byte-identical to the source-verified copy at `stu-reference/f66cc8b7e3555676.png`. `apple-touch-icon.png` uses the matching 500 × 500 original from `school-assets/5e2bccf79181a67fd811.png`. The captured school assets and their source/checksum manifests remain under those folders. `direct-school.css` styles the standalone school pages and floating assistant control; `direct-school-assistant.js` handles only closing the same-origin assistant popover. Captured source scripts remain excluded.

`stu-reference/` also contains the 16 public School Affairs login photos. Their source URLs, local paths and SHA-256 checksums are listed in its `provenance.json`.
