# STU Nexus video guides

Six narrated chapters combine explanatory Remotion graphics with recordings of the actual client portal. The teacher and student chapters are the main onboarding guides; the overview is a short project pitch. The remaining chapters cover every other platform role.

| Chapter | Audience and workflow |
| --- | --- |
| 01 · Project pitch | Connected learning, support and human-reviewed AI |
| 02 · Teacher | Classes, lesson editing/publication, quizzes, written work/feedback, AI grade scan/review/publication, attendance, dates, announcements and Assistant |
| 03 · Student | Lessons/completion, quizzes/results, assignment submission, published grades, calendar, Assistant and contact draft |
| 04 · Academics | Registrar, department head and dean: curriculum, classes/enrollment, change proposals and scheduling |
| 05 · Student services | Advisor, admissions, finance and support: student context, AI plan, assigned case, prospect workflow and message draft |
| 06 · Leadership and access | President, vice president, librarian, admin and owner: reports, evidence limits, reviewed knowledge, accounts and role preview |

Outputs live in ignored `artifacts/tutorials/exports/`: six MP4s and a combined chaptered master, matching SRT/VTT files, chapter timestamps and a local viewing gallery. Narration is in English, using ElevenLabs Matilda. Captions are also burned into each video. Delivery and editable-source/assets ZIP archives are generated by the package command. All recordings use demonstration accounts and synthetic records. Pauses may be shortened; AI drafts and unsent messages are described as drafts.

[Setup and rendering](SETUP.md) · [Source structure and boundaries](PROJECT.md) · [QA evidence and limitations](../../docs/qa/VIDEO_GUIDES.md).

The web application does not depend on this package. The ElevenLabs credential belongs only in the ignored root `.env.elevenlabs.local`, with mode `0600`; it must never be bundled, exposed through Vite or committed.
